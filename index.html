Designed for Emotional Safety

StillMatters isn't another productivity app. It's emotional sustainability software for helpers who rarely see their long-term impact.

💚

Quick Logging

Tap an emoji category, choose a color, add an optional note. Takes 10 seconds, not 10 minutes.

🫁

Breathing Exercise

3 slow, guided breaths after each entry. Supporting your nervous system is part of self-care.

📅

Weekly Summaries

Gentle reflections that show your impact without comparison or judgment.

🎨

Calming Design

Soft colors, gentle shapes, affirming language. No harsh alerts, no pressure, no guilt.

🚨

Emergency Erase

Instantly delete all entries. No confirmation screens. Critical for workers in sensitive situations.

✨

No Streaks, No Shame

No missed day reminders. No productivity scores. No gamification of your emotional labor.

🔒 Your Privacy is Sacred

We believe emotional safety requires data safety. That's why StillMatters collects absolutely nothing.

❌ No Accounts

No sign-up, no login, no email required

❌ No Cloud Storage

Everything stays on your device only

❌ No Tracking

No analytics, no data collection, period

✅ Your Data

You own it, you control it, you delete it
